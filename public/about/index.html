About

I am relentlessly curious about humanity.

Before I became a marketer, I was a professional violinist, a trusts lawyer, a financial analyst, a salesperson, a software tester. I was genuinely curious about each of those worlds - what they could teach me about people and problems.

Within growth and marketing, I made the same choice deliberately. Brand, paid, partnerships, B2B, B2C, growth, different industries - I wanted to understand the whole craft, not just a corner of it. I want to build a block-shaped profile. Wide enough to see across a problem. Deep enough to be useful.

My violin professor used to say: in most professions, the top 20-30% can make a very good living. In music, you have to be in the top 0.01%. I think AI is bringing that reality to most professions. I am drawn to excellence, and want to be surrounded by other people who want the same thing. At the same time, I believe deeply in the potential of the human being - that is who I want to build for. For me, the most important work is making sure the person on the other side of the screen isn’t lost in the process.

I write about growth, marketing and other things that draw my curiosity. Underpinning it all is humanism.
