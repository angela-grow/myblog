About

I lead global B2B and B2C growth at a Series-E fintech, operating across 7 international markets and managing an 8-figure media budget. Here, I’ve driven 60% YoY revenue growth alongside a 30% EBITDA increase.

With AI, I’m increasingly building growth marketing using agentic workflows to move faster and personalize better.

I write about growth, marketing and where I think it’s all heading. I’m also available for fractional and advisory work - see how I can help.
